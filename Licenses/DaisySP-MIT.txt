StageKeys adaptation scope
--------------------------
The Stage Chorus implementation in Sources/Synth.c adapts DaisySP Chorus's
MIT-licensed triangle-LFO modulated-delay foundation into an independent
two-voice stereo processor using StageKeys' preallocated delay storage,
parameter queue, gain staging and feedback protection.
